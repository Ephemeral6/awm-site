Rule stability
--------------

The game's action semantics are the same on every level: one and the same piece of code
resolves your actions throughout. So a rule about *how an action resolves* - what a direction
key does, how the panel responds to a click, what a completed pattern does, what happens when
two things meet, what an action costs - holds on every level once it holds on one. You do not
need to re-establish such a rule after a level change, and "I only ever tested this on the
previous level" is not by itself a reason to doubt it.

What each level carries of its own is only its data: which objects are present and where they
sit, which of the abilities that level enables, and the numeric values that belong to that
level. Those you do have to read off the level you are on.
