## b. Rules (as functions; all are `stepBase`/`step` in Lean)
- R-move: mv d means k = the largest k ≤ sz whose shifted sz×sz square is all floor (0 = blocked); move by k; face := d; cost 1. Evidence: steps of 4 and 2, the L1 clamp (moved 2), blocked moves still turn the player.
- R-win: after a move, the square overlaps the goal interior and the game is not over → won.
- R-click: toggle panel cell i; cost 1.
- R-plus: the marks become the plus pattern → sz := 2 (top-left kept), marks cleared, cost 2.
- R-col: the marks become the middle column → marks cleared, cost 2, and the barrier is removed iff `near s`. `near` is NOT modelled: it is a parameter. Observed true at (13,29) sz4 (L3) and at (9,31) sz2 (L4). Observed false at (17,37) facing down (L3), and at (21,37) and (21,29) sz4 facing up (L4).
- R-timer (new this attempt): cost per accepted action is 1, a recipe completion costs 2, no-op clicks cost 0. Display rows = 64 − 2·floor(32u/B). over iff u > B. Fit by brute force: B = 50/25/50/35 for L1/L2/L3/L4. It reproduces every observed bar reading, including all 31 in L4, and GAME_OVER exactly at u = 36.
- Not in the model: the first action after a reset can be swallowed (cost 0, no effect); the 2×2 colour-14 tile in L4 (treated as plain floor: looser); clicks outside the panel (no-ops, cost 0, omitted: looser).
