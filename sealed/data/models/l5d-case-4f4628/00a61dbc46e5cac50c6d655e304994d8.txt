# Formal system, level 5 (attempt 1). Lean: lean/L5Model.lean

## a. Objects (each slot: which two positions it separates)
- Piece: t,l (top-left px), s (4|2), f (facing U/D/L/R). The four slots keep apart:
  - t/l: different positions;
  - s: the same top-left at 4x4 vs 2x2 (it fits the stem or not);
  - f: the same square facing U vs R (the beam from the front-left strip differs, the shrink anchor differs).
- Barrier C open / barrier D open (co, dO): the same piece position with the barrier passable or not.
- Pickup taken (pk): the same position with +9 still available or not.
- Energy e and action counter n: e separates "next action allowed" from GAME_OVER. n separates free from paid ticks.
- won / over: terminal flags.
- Static (constants, not state): floor rectangles, barC, barD, swC, swD, goal, pick.
  Teleport targets, free-tick period, surcharge, refill and start energy are Params (fitted, not established).
- Panel: not a state slot. Clicks only change the panel until a pattern is completed (seen in 40 grid_click
  transitions), and completion resets it. So a spell = an atomic macro of k clicks, charged k actions.

## b. Rules (Lean defs)
- M (`step .mv` + `tryMove`): move by the largest k ≤ s whose window is passable; facing := dir.
  Pickup: +refill on first touch. Win if the window touches the goal.
- B: no k fits → facing := dir only.
- SH (`.shrink`): s=4 ∧ f=U → s:=2 at the same top-left.
- S6 (`.six`/`beam`): a 2-wide beam from the front-left strip travels through floor.
  If all its cells land in swC (or swD), barrier C (or D) opens. Otherwise nothing happens.
- T (`.tele`): the piece moves to tele4/tele2 by size.
- E (`pay`): game over if an action is attempted with e=0. Each primitive costs 1, but 0 if n%freeMod=1.
  The completing click costs +surcharge.

## c. Simplify / check against observations
I replayed the 30 real L5 macro steps (42 primitive actions) through the Lean `step`:
- piece position/size/facing and the barrier flags match at every step;
- energy matches at every step except after action 21 (model 12, observed 11), and it re-syncs at action 22.
  The free tick was observed at 22, but the model puts it at 21.
Slots read by no rule: none dropped (`over` is read by step).
